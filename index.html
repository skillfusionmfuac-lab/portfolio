Who I Am

About Me

As a dedicated university lecturer, I am passionate about bridging the gap between complex theoretical concepts and real-world scientific application. With a strong background in chemistry, I strive to create dynamic, inclusive classrooms and laboratories that encourage active participation and critical thinking.

My teaching philosophy is grounded in the belief that education is a collaborative journey. Rather than simply conveying foundational chemical principles, I focus on helping students develop the analytical and experimental skills required to solve complex problems and innovate within the sciences.

Before entering academia full-time, I worked in applied chemical research — gaining practical laboratory and industry experience that I now bring directly into my teaching. My goal is to empower the next generation of scientists to approach challenges with curiosity, confidence, creativity, and a passion for lifelong learning.

Pedagogy

Teaching Philosophy

I believe that great teaching goes beyond the transmission of information. Science education, at its best, invites students to question, experiment, and discover. My approach centers on creating environments where students feel both challenged and supported — where making mistakes is a natural part of scientific growth.

01

Collaborative Learning

Science thrives in community. I foster teamwork, peer discussion, and shared problem-solving across all learning environments.

02

Critical Thinking

Students are encouraged to question assumptions, analyze evidence carefully, and form well-reasoned scientific conclusions.

03

Laboratory Learning

Hands-on experimentation bridges the gap between theory and practice, making abstract concepts tangible and meaningful.

04

Scientific Curiosity

Nurturing a genuine love of discovery — helping students see chemistry not just as a subject, but as a way of understanding the world.

05

Inclusive Education

Every student deserves access to meaningful science education, regardless of their background or prior experience.

06

Lifelong Learning

My greatest goal is for students to leave my courses not just with knowledge, but with the desire and ability to keep learning independently.
